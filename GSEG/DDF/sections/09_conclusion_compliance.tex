\section{9. Conclusion and Design Compliance Status}

\subsection{9.1 Design Conclusion}

The GSEG preliminary design definition is sufficiently structured to guide the next level of subsystem specifications and detailed design. It is \textbf{not yet ready for formal design closure}: proposed allocations, external interfaces and integrated-performance capabilities still require controlled confirmation. Completion of a DDF working draft does not constitute verification or approval for operation.
