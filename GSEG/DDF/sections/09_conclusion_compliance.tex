\section{9. Conclusion and Design Compliance
Status}\label{conclusion-and-design-compliance-status}

\subsection{9.1 Design Maturity
Conclusion}\label{design-maturity-conclusion}

The preliminary GSEG design definition is sufficiently structured to
serve as a basis for lower-level specifications and designs. \textbf{It
is not yet ready for formal design closure:} proposed allocations,
interfaces and properties of the integrated segment still require
agreement and confirmation. Completion of a working DDF is not proof of
compliance or authorization for operation.
