\section{1. Introduction and Scope}

\subsection{1.1 Purpose}

This Design Definition File (DDF) defines the preliminary design of the EWAS Ground Segment (GSEG, CI-02) at integrated-segment level. It establishes the functional and physical architecture, product and specification trees, interface boundaries, technical allocations and design constraints for subsequent subsystem development.

The GSEG Technical Requirements Specification (TRS) is the source of the applicable requirements. Design rationale, trade studies and substantiation are addressed in the Design Justification File (DDJF), not repeated here.

\subsection{1.2 Product Scope}

The GSEG product comprises seven subsystems: Data Archiving System (DAS), Command \& Control (C\&C), Mission Control Center (MCC), Data Processing System (DPS), Ground Station Network (GSN), Early Warning System (EWS) and Ground Infrastructure Subsystem (GIS).

This DDF defines their integrated arrangement and segment-level allocations. Internal subsystem design is specified in the respective lower-level DDFs.

\subsection{1.3 Design Maturity}

This document describes a preliminary, controlled working design. \textbf{B} identifies values established by the applicable requirements baseline; \textbf{P} identifies proposed design-to allocations that remain subject to approval and substantiation. Neither inclusion in this DDF nor the absence of a recorded nonconformance constitutes evidence of compliance.
