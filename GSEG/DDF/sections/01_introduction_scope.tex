\section{1. Introduction and Scope}\label{introduction-and-scope}

\subsection{1.1. Purpose of the Document}\label{purpose-of-the-document}

The Design Definition File presents the design solution for the integrated GSEG (CI-02, WBS 123456.02): its functional and physical architecture, product, interfaces, parameters, constraints and completeness status. The \textbf{TRS} is the source of requirements, and the \textbf{DDJF} provides their justification. The DDF itself does not establish new parent-level requirements.

\subsection{1.2. Product Scope}\label{product-scope}

GSEG comprises DAS, C\&C, MCC, DPS, GSN, EWS and GIS. Internal subsystem responsibilities will be developed in lower-level documents; common interfaces must remain consistent with this solution and the ICDs. SSEG and warning recipients beyond the adopted interfaces are not part of the GSEG product.

\subsection{1.3. Maturity}\label{design-maturity}

The following designations are adopted: \textbf{B} — value derived from current TRS or parent requirements; \textbf{P} — proposed design allocation, without approved evidence; \textbf{A/\allowbreak{}V} — only after presentation of analysis or verification evidence. No P value has been assigned A/\allowbreak{}V status here.
