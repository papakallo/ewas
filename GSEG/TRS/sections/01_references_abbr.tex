Applicable Documents (\abbr{AD}) provide the project requirements and selected engineering framework. Reference Documents (\abbr{RD}) provide supporting technical information. ECSS applicability is subject to project-specific tailoring.

\subsection{Terms, Definitions and Abbreviations}

The terminology in \textbf{AD-01} and \textbf{RD-04} applies. The following project-specific terms are used in this specification.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Candidate Event:} A possible missile launch detected by a \abbr{GEO} satellite and reported to the Ground Segment for further observation and assessment.
  \item \textbf{Onboard Preliminary Trajectory:} An initial three-dimensional trajectory estimate derived from stereoscopic observations exchanged by the selected \abbr{LEO} spacecraft and transferred to the Ground Segment.
  \item \textbf{Ground Multi-Sensor Fusion:} Combination of available observations and the onboard preliminary trajectory to generate a refined trajectory and probabilistic impact-area estimate on the ground.
  \item \textbf{Probabilistic Impact Area:} A predicted geographical impact region presented with a specified confidence level and uncertainty description.
  \item \textbf{Preliminary Warning:} The first warning automatically delivered to an authorized authority interface following the required ground assessment of observations from two selected \abbr{LEO} spacecraft.
\end{itemize}
