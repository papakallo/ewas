spacecraft and reported for further assessment. \\ \hline
Onboard Preliminary Trajectory & Preliminary trajectory estimated from
stereoscopic observations by the selected LEO spacecraft. \\ \hline
Ground Multi-Sensor Fusion & Combination of observations and a
preliminary trajectory to estimate a refined trajectory and
probabilistic impact area. \\ \hline
Probabilistic Impact Area & Predicted geographic impact region with a
specified confidence level and uncertainty information. \\ \hline
Preliminary Warning & First warning delivered automatically after ground
assessment of data from two LEO spacecraft. \\*
