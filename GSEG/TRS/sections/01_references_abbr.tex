The definitions below supplement the terminology in \texttt{AD-01} and \texttt{RD-04}.

\thead{Term} & \thead{Definition} \\ \hline
\endfirsthead
\hline\rowcolor{TableHeaderBlue}
\thead{Term} & \thead{Definition} \\ \hline
\endhead
\textbf{Candidate Event} & A potential missile launch detected by a GEO spacecraft and reported for further assessment. \\ \hline
\textbf{Onboard Preliminary Trajectory} & An initial trajectory estimate derived from stereoscopic observations by the selected LEO spacecraft and transferred to GSEG. \\ \hline
\textbf{Ground Multi-Sensor Fusion} & The combination of relevant observations and the onboard preliminary trajectory to calculate a refined trajectory and probabilistic impact region. \\ \hline
\textbf{Probabilistic Impact Area} & A predicted geographical impact region accompanied by a specified confidence level and information on estimation uncertainty. \\ \hline
\textbf{Preliminary Warning} & The first warning automatically delivered to an authorized recipient after the ground assessment of observations from two selected LEO spacecraft. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}}
