\section{Annex A: Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)}

allocation of the 35 GSEG requirements. It is an engineering working trace,
not an approved lower-level requirements baseline. Verified child-requirement
identifiers and evidence will be added when available. Cross-cutting allocations
do not establish exclusive implementation ownership.
