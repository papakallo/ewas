\section{Annex A. Requirements Traceability Matrix
(RTM)}\label{annex-a.-requirements-traceability-matrix-rtm}

\textbf{Status: preliminary matrix --- informative allocations;
subsystem TRSs not yet approved.} The matrix uses
the ``Basis'' column in Chapter 7 and the functional/interface
allocation in DDF Chapters 4--5. It adds no new requirements; functional
owners are preliminary allocation points only.

\textbf{Matrix scope:} links to future subsystem TRS requirements,
configuration identifiers and verification evidence remain open. In
particular, cross-cutting rows do not establish exclusive implementation
ownership.
