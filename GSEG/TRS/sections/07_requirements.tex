\section{7. GSEG Technical Requirements (Normative)}\label{gseg-technical-requirements}

\textbf{Note:} exactly 35 identifiers and their associated verification methods from GSEG TRS Issue 0.7 have been retained; the statements are translated from the Polish editorial version, not a new issue of the requirements baseline.

\subsection{7.1. Functional Requirements}\label{functional-and-mission-requirements}

\begingroup\GSEGTableStyle{4}
\begin{longtable}{|P{0.27}|P{0.46}|P{0.16}|P{0.11}|}
\hline\rowcolor{TableHeaderBlue}
\GSEGHeaderText{Identifier and title} & \GSEGHeaderText{Requirement} & \GSEGHeaderText{Source} & \GSEGHeaderText{Method}\\ \hline
\endhead
\hline\endlastfoot
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-001 — Selection of Two LEO Spacecraft} & Upon receipt of a GEO Event Report, GSEG shall select two LEO spacecraft for follow-up observations based on event location, observation geometry, spacecraft status and communications availability. & R-043 & T, A\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-002 — Observation Request Handling} & GSEG shall generate requests for both selected LEO spacecraft, hand them over to the SSEG communications interface and make transmission and execution status available. & R-030, R-044 & T\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-003 — Ground Event Classification} & GSEG shall automatically distinguish a potential threat, an ambiguous event in which a credible threat cannot be excluded, and a credibly non-threatening event. If the required data are missing, it shall indicate the distinct “assessment impossible” status without assigning a threat class. & R-019, R-013 & T, A\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-004 — Authoritative Observation Fusion} & GSEG shall combine timestamped LOS observations and uncertainty data from two selected LEO spacecraft and the onboard preliminary trajectory to obtain a refined trajectory and probabilistic impact area. & R-012, R-026, R-027 & T, A\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-005 — Automatic First Warning} & Following receipt and valid assessment of observations from two LEO spacecraft and determination of the trajectory and impact area, GSEG shall automatically deliver the first warning if there is a potential threat or if an ambiguous result does not exclude a credible threat. The warning shall include uncertainty. No external warning shall be sent in the absence of a threat or when the status is “assessment impossible”. Prior operator approval is not required. & R-011, R-013, R-045 & T\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-006 — Event Assessment Updates} & GSEG shall update the trajectory and impact area following receipt of further observations associated with an active event. & R-014 & T\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-007 — Warning Revision and Cancellation} & GSEG shall enable an authorized operator to confirm, revise or cancel a previously sent warning and transmit the corresponding update. & R-046 & T\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-008 — Baltic Sea Region Prioritization} & GSEG shall assess events originating throughout the global surveillance area for threats to the Baltic Sea region and prioritize the relevant warnings. & R-049 & T, A\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-FUN-009 — Exclusion of Offensive Functions} & GSEG shall not provide weapon guidance, fire control, interceptor control or directly control military response assets. & R-001 & R, I\\*
\end{longtable}

\textbf{GSEG-REQ-PERF-001 — Two-LEO Coordination Time} & The interval from receipt of the GEO report by GSN to handover of requests for both selected LEO spacecraft at the GSEG/\allowbreak{}SSEG boundary, including internal data transfer and selection, shall not exceed \textbf{2 s}. & R-011, R-043 & T, A\\ \hline
\textbf{GSEG-REQ-PERF-002 — Assessment and Warning Delivery Time} & For events qualified for warning, the interval from GSN receipt of the complete set of two LEO products and the preliminary trajectory to delivery of the warning at the agreed recipient interface, including validation, fusion, the decision and dissemination, shall not exceed \textbf{15 s}. & R-011, R-045 & T, A\\*

\textbf{GSEG-REQ-DATA-002 — Input Data Validation} & Before using data in an assessment that can trigger the first warning, GSEG shall check that the packages correspond to the event and contain all data and metadata required for fusion. & R-012, R-027, R-045 & T\\ \hline

\textbf{GSEG-REQ-DATA-005 — Historical Data Retention} & GSEG shall retain mission data for \textbf{at least 5 years}, preserving integrity and availability to authorized users. & R-036 & A, T\\ \hline
