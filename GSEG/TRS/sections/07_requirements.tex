\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}001}} --- Selection of Two LEO Spacecraft &
Upon receipt of a GEO Event Report, GSEG \textbf{shall} select two LEO
spacecraft for follow-up observation based on event location,
observation geometry, spacecraft status and communications availability.
& R-043 & T, A \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}002}} --- Observation Request Handling &
GSEG \textbf{shall} generate requests for both selected LEO spacecraft,
hand them over to the SSEG communications interface, and make
transmission and execution status available. & R-030, R-044 & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}003}} --- Ground Event Classification &
GSEG \textbf{shall} automatically distinguish (a) an event constituting
a potential threat, (b) an ambiguous event for which a credible threat
cannot be excluded, and (c) a credibly non-threatening event. If the
input data do not permit the required assessment, GSEG \textbf{shall}
indicate the distinct \textbf{``assessment impossible''} status instead
of assigning a threat class. & R-019, R-013 & T, A \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}004}} --- Authoritative Ground Data Fusion
& GSEG \textbf{shall} fuse timestamped stereoscopic LOS observations,
their uncertainty metadata and the onboard preliminary trajectory from
two selected LEO spacecraft to produce a refined trajectory and
probabilistic impact area. & R-012, R-026, R-027 & T, A \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}005}} --- Automatic First Preliminary
Warning & Following receipt and valid assessment of observations from
two selected LEO spacecraft and estimation of the refined trajectory and
impact area, GSEG \textbf{shall automatically} deliver the first
preliminary warning if the assessment indicates a potential threat or
remains ambiguous without excluding a credible threat. The warning
\textbf{shall} convey appropriate uncertainty information. GSEG
\textbf{shall not} issue such a warning for credibly non-threatening
events or when the status is ``assessment impossible''. Prior operator
approval is not required. & R-011, R-013, R-045 & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}006}} --- Event Assessment Updates & GSEG
\textbf{shall} update the refined trajectory and impact-area assessment
upon receipt of additional observation data associated with an active
event. & R-014 & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}007}} --- Warning Revision and Cancellation
& GSEG \textbf{shall} enable an authorized operator to confirm, revise
or cancel a previously delivered warning and transmit the corresponding
update. & R-046 & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}008}} --- Baltic Region Threat
Prioritization & GSEG \textbf{shall} assess events originating
throughout the global surveillance area for threats to the Baltic region
and prioritize the relevant warning products. & R-049 & T, A \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-\allowbreak{}REQ-\allowbreak{}FUN-\allowbreak{}009}} --- Non-Offensive Scope & GSEG
\textbf{shall not} provide weapon guidance, fire control, interceptor
control or direct control of military response assets. & R-001 & R, I \\*
\end{longtable}
