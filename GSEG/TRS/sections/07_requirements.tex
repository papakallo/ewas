\subsection{7. Technical Requirements}

This section specifies the normative technical requirements for the integrated
Ground Segment (GSEG, \texttt{CI-02}). Requirements derive from the EWAS
system baseline, the operational concept, segment boundaries, and the agreed
Space Segment interfaces. Allocation to individual Ground Segment subsystems
will be defined in subordinate specifications and the Requirements Traceability
Matrix (RTM). The approved product architecture comprises seven subsystems,
including the Ground Infrastructure Subsystem (GIS). GIS supports common
computing, networks, time distribution and on-site utilities; MCC owns
flight-dynamics and mission planning, while C\&C retains automatic two-LEO
selection and command handling. This paragraph states the allocation context
and does not introduce new normative requirement IDs.

Each requirement has a persistent identifier, a parent requirement or approved
segment-level basis, and planned verification methods: Test (T), Analysis (A),
Review of Design (R), and Inspection (I). Verification methods do not imply
that compliance has already been demonstrated.

The 60-second end-to-end limit applies to the complete EWAS system
(\texttt{R-011}); the Ground Segment timing allocations are 2 seconds for
LEO selection and tracking-request handover and 15 seconds for ground
assessment and warning delivery for warning-eligible events. The system availability requirement
exceeding 99.9\% (\texttt{R-015}) is not an independently established
numerical availability allocation for GSEG.

\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-REQ-FUN-005}}\newline \emph{Automatic First Preliminary Warning} & After receiving and validly assessing observations from both selected LEO spacecraft and producing a refined trajectory and impact-area estimate, the Ground Segment \textbf{shall automatically deliver} a first preliminary warning when (a) a potential threat is assessed or (b) the classification is indeterminate and a credible threat cannot be excluded. The warning \textbf{shall} convey the corresponding uncertainty. No first preliminary warning \textbf{shall} be issued for a credibly non-threatening event or \emph{Assessment Unavailable}. Delivery \textbf{shall} not require prior operator approval. & \texttt{R-011}, \texttt{R-013}, \texttt{R-045} & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-REQ-FUN-006}}\newline \emph{Event Assessment Updates} & The Ground Segment \textbf{shall} update the refined trajectory and impact-area assessment upon receipt of additional tracking data associated with an active event. & \texttt{R-014} & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-REQ-FUN-007}}\newline \emph{Warning Revision and Cancellation} & The Ground Segment \textbf{shall} enable an authorized operator to confirm, revise, or cancel a previously delivered warning and to transmit the corresponding update message. & \texttt{R-046} & T \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-REQ-FUN-008}}\newline \emph{Baltic Region Threat Prioritization} & The Ground Segment \textbf{shall} assess events originating throughout the global surveillance area for potential threats to the Baltic region and prioritize the corresponding warning products. & \texttt{R-049} & T, A \\ \hline
\textbf{\texttt{GSEG-REQ-FUN-009}}\newline \emph{Non-Offensive Functional Scope} & The Ground Segment \textbf{shall} not provide weapon-guidance, fire-control, interceptor-control, or direct military-response asset control functions. & \texttt{R-001} & R, I \\ \hline
