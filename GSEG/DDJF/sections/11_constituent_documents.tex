\section{11. Constituent Documents}\label{ddjf-11}

\subsection{11.1. Principles for Compiling Documents}\label{ddjf-11-1}

In accordance with ECSS-E-ST-10C (6 March 2009), Annex K, Chapter 11, the DDJF should contain or reference documents containing option decisions, analyses, requirement justification, traceability and verification results. This list distinguishes \textbf{existing and reviewed material}, \textbf{documents listed in TRS without confirmed availability}, and \textbf{evidence still to be developed}. Recording an item alone does not mean its issue, qualification or approval.

\subsection{11.4. Lower-Level Documentation}\label{ddjf-11-4}

The GSEG DDJF should reference appropriate documents and reports for GSN, C\&C, DPS, EWS, MCC, DAS and GIS, particularly where equipment and implementation data are required. Complete, approved DDJFs for these subsystems have not been confirmed at this stage. Their existence must not be simulated in the list by adding issue numbers.

After lower-level documentation is received, a list of its identifiers, current versions, requirements inherited from GSEG, analyses and unresolved deviations must be incorporated into this chapter.

\subsection{11.5. Evidence Register to Be Obtained}\label{ddjf-11-5}

Chapter 7 contains the list of analyses \textbf{GSEG-AN-01–09}, and Chapter 8 the tests \textbf{GSEG-QV-01–12}. The key new evidence is reserve LEO geometry and activation (AN-03/\allowbreak{}QV-11) and performance of priority allocation with saturation of 10 slots (AN-04/\allowbreak{}QV-12). Formal compliance requires links to reports in VCD.
