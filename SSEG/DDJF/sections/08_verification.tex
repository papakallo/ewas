\section{Verification Activities and Synthesis of Results}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Verification Plan}
\label{sec:verification_plan}

The Space Segment verification strategy is governed by the Programme Verification Plan (\textbf{AD-02}) and tracked via the Verification Control Document (\textbf{AD-03}). Four formal verification methods are employed, assigned per requirement in the TRS (\textbf{RD-02}):

{\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}%
\arrayrulecolor{TableBorderBlue}%
\rowcolors{2}{white}{TableRowLightBlue}%
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{1.5cm}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.5cm}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X|}
    \hline
    \rowcolor{TableHeaderBlue}
    \thead{Code} & \thead{Method} & \thead{Application at SSEG Level} \\ \hline
    \textbf{T} & Test & Environmental qualification (vibration, acoustic, TVAC, shock, EMC); functional end-to-end chain tests; radiometric calibration; OISL BER validation. \\ \hline
    \textbf{A} & Analysis & Orbital coverage (GMAT), radiation dose (AP-8/AE-8, IRENE AE9/AP9), thermal modelling, structural FEM, link budgets, $\Delta v$ budgets, mass/power budgets. \\ \hline
    \textbf{R} & Review of Design & Non-offensive scope (\texttt{R-001}), data-format compliance, design-for-demise compliance, COMSEC architecture review. \\ \hline
    \textbf{I} & Inspection & Physical configuration audit, harness routing, cleanroom contamination logs, material outgassing certificates. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}}

The verification programme is structured into three campaigns:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{EM/STM Campaign:} Functional HIL testing and structural/thermal model correlation.
    \item \textbf{QM/PFM Campaign:} Full environmental qualification of lead SATL and SATG.
    \item \textbf{FM Acceptance Campaign:} Workmanship screening and payload calibration of all 72 SATL and 6 SATG flight units.
\end{enumerate}
