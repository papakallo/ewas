\section{Verification Activities and Synthesis of Results}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Verification Governance and Strategy Synthesis}
\label{sec:verification_governance}

The formal verification strategy, methodology, model philosophy, and execution requirements for the \abbr{EWAS} Space Segment (\texttt{CI-03}, \abbr{WBS} Element \texttt{123456.03}) are governed normatively by the Programme Verification Plan (\textbf{AD-04}, \texttt{EWAS-HSB-VP-01}). Requirement-by-requirement verification tracking, execution evidence close-out, and qualification status monitoring are maintained dynamically in the Programme Verification Control Document (\textbf{AD-05}, \texttt{EWAS-HSB-VCD-01}).

In accordance with \abbr{ECSS}-E-ST-10-02C Annex~K Clause~\textless8\textgreater, this section provides the synthesis of engineering justification results and presents the formal \textbf{Requirements Status Log} for all 39 Level-2 Space Segment technical requirements established in \textbf{AD-01} (\texttt{EWAS-HSB-SSEG-TRS-03} Issue~1.5).

\subsection{Space Segment Requirements Status Log}
\label{sec:req_status_log_section}

The requirements baseline for \texttt{CI-03} comprises 39 normative requirements across 9 functional and technical categories. In Table~\ref{tab:req_status_log}, the baseline is partitioned into two distinct verification categories:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Category 1: Requirements Justified Analytically within this DDJF (16 Requirements):} Requirements where architectural design review, astrodynamic modelling, radiometric SNR derivation, or technical budget allocations (mass, power, stiffness, $\Delta v$, radiation, latency, and bandwidth) provide complete Phase~A/B design justification.
    \item \textbf{Category 2: Requirements Tracked in the Verification Control Document (23 Requirements):} Requirements requiring empirical qualification testing (environmental campaigns, vibration, TVAC, shock, EMC), hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) functional test bench execution, physical inspection, or in-orbit commissioning. These requirements remain open during Phase~A/B and are actively tracked to close-out in \textbf{AD-05} (\texttt{EWAS-HSB-VCD-01}).
\end{enumerate}
