\subsubsection{5.5 Description of Interfaces}

This subsection defines the functional and physical boundaries of the Space Segment across four external interfaces (\texttt{IF-EXT-01} to \texttt{IF-EXT-04}) and three internal space-segment interface classes (\texttt{IF-INT-01} to \texttt{IF-INT-03}). Detailed packet schemas, connector pinouts, and mechanical interface drawings are controlled at lower levels in the Space Segment Interface Control Document (\textbf{RD-06}, \abbr{WBS} \texttt{123456.05.02}) and the subordinate spacecraft design files (\textbf{RD-04} and \textbf{RD-05}).

\paragraph{External Interfaces (\texttt{IF-EXT-01} to \texttt{IF-EXT-04}).}
All direct communications between the Space Segment and Earth use all-weather radio frequencies rather than optical lasers, ensuring uninterrupted command and warning delivery through European cloud cover and precipitation. Because the \abbr{EWAS} architecture comprises strictly two system segments---the Ground Segment (\texttt{123456.02}) and the Space Segment (\texttt{123456.03})---launch service providers and space-surveillance networks are managed exclusively as external interfaces rather than additional segments. \paragraph{Internal Space Segment Interfaces (\texttt{IF-INT-01} to \texttt{IF-INT-03}).}

Within each spacecraft, the mechanical, thermal, electrical, and data boundary between the bus platform and mission payload is standardized across both flight-system lines and controlled in detail at Level-3. \paragraph{Interface Standards and Timing Governance.}
All external \abbr{RF} links and internal \abbr{OISL} channels adhere to uniform \abbr{CCSDS} packet and frame formatting to ensure seamless end-to-end routing between the Ground and Space Segments. Constellation-wide time synchronization is maintained to better than $< 50~\mu\mathrm{s}$ relative to \abbr{UTC} via onboard \abbr{GNSS} receivers so that paired \texttt{SATL} observations remain temporally aligned for valid stereoscopic 3D triangulation. Hardware-enforced \abbr{COMSEC} bulk encryption and cryptographic command authentication protect every external and inter-satellite link against interception and spoofing. All Space Segment interface baselines, protocol profiles, and verification checkpoints are maintained under formal configuration control in the Space Segment Interface Control Document \textbf{RD-06} (\texttt{EWAS-HSB-SSEG-ICD-03}, \abbr{WBS} \texttt{123456.05.02}).
