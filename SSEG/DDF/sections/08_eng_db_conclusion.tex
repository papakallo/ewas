\subsection{9. Conclusion}

\paragraph{Baseline Compliance Assessment.}
The Level-2 Space Segment as-designed baseline defined in \texttt{EWAS-HSB-SSEG-DDF-03} satisfies all 39 normative requirements of \textbf{AD-01} (\texttt{EWAS-HSB-SSEG-TRS-03} Issue~1.5) with \textbf{zero technical deviations or waivers}. Every sizing and performance budget closes with positive Phase~A/B reserve across mass, electrical power, propulsion $\Delta v$, stowed structural stiffness, optical and radio-frequency link closure, pointing accuracy, radiation shielding, and the $\le 60\text{ s}$ civil-protection warning chain. Complete one-to-one traceability from requirements to implementing design elements and Phase~A/B compliance status is provided in Annex~B (formal verification tracking resides in \textbf{AD-03}/\textbf{AD-04}).

\paragraph{Readiness for Level-3 Design and Open Items.}
The Space Segment baseline is mature for Preliminary Design Review (\abbr{PDR}) progression and direct flow-down into the subordinate \texttt{SATL} (\textbf{RD-02}, \textbf{RD-04}) and \texttt{SATG} (\textbf{RD-03}, \textbf{RD-05}) spacecraft baselines.
